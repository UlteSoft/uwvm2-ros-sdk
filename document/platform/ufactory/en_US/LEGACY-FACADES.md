# ufactory: retained complete helper workflows

`c/state.h` retains all 55 original xArm/Lite6/UF850 model/profile selections and exact DOF. The descriptor maps an explicit model selector to the original `UWVM_XARM_SELECTION`. `uwvm_xarm_accept_observation` validates the whole original RobotMsg and exact selected joint count before committing output. `uwvm_xarm_poll_state` polls once; `uwvm_xarm_query_once` calls the exact original read-only service once and preserves all native result/error fields. `cpp/state.hpp` supplies borrowed `Reader.poll` and `QueryClient.call` wrappers. Joint angles retain radians; other pose/offset units remain those of the selected definition.

`js/robot_states.mjs` exports `RobotStateObserver`: `onJointStates`, `onRobotStates`, `onCgpioStates`, `close`. The original Humble-Gazebo JointState, RobotMsg and CIOState callbacks and logger text are retained. Even a notification-only callback validates every original field. `js/core/humble/bounded_records.mjs` retains the complete standard support records used by that workflow, including JointState/Header/time and trajectory/multi-array values. Deterministic text formatting preserves the original finite binary64 fixed-six domain, including signed zero. The caller supplies an actual byte-output function; output failure remains an error and local closure creates no native unsubscribe or motion command.

All helpers retain caller-owned native capabilities. Use the exact source profile and complete generated record/interface headers. Preserve primary operation, whole-value decoding, application response and one independent close as distinct facts. Fatal process/trap cleanup and physical controller completion are outside local facade state. Genuine x86 native ROS and QEMU ARM evidence is required separately; these source/manuals do not certify workflow or hardware parity.
